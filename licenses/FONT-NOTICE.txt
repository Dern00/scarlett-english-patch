ScarlettFont is derived from EdensRitterEN, itself modified from Bree by TypeTogether and Sawarabi Mincho by mshio.
The original font credits and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are retained in the accompanying license files.
ScarlettFont restores the Latin letter ô as an o/circumflex composite and maps the original note outline to U+266A (♪). All other existing outlines, metrics and character mappings are unchanged.
Original EdensRitterEN file remains unchanged. The patch loads ScarlettFont privately inside its process.
